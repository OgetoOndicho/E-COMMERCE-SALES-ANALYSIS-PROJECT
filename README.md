# E-COMMERCE-SALES-ANALYSIS-PROJECT
This is an end to end Sales Analytics project for an E-Commerce Franchise. This project fully utilizes the power of Spreadsheets (Microsoft Excel) to explore and uncover more insights about the data.

## PROJECT OVERVIEW
The stakeholders at Trademark Global; an E-commerce retail store are interested to know how sales of products perform at stores around the world. The data analyst is expected to provide a comprehensive report about sales revenue and profits on their products.

## BUSINESS OBJECTIVES
- To investigate the products that generate the highest revenues and profits in sales.
- To uncover trends and patterns in the financial metrics of costs, revenue and profits over time.
- To investigate if there is any relationship between the delivery time and order status (Whether completed or returned).
- Which is the preferred mode of payment by customers during transactions of goods.

## ABOUT THE DATA
The data consisted of 500 rows and 8 rows at the beginning. However the number adjusted gradually due to data cleaning and analysis.
The original dataset comprised of Key columns namely: 
- Customer Names,
- Product Category
- Product Name	Order
- Date	Delivered Date
- Quantity
- Unit Price
- Status
- Country of origin
- Payment Method
Dataset Source: Obtained from Kaggle...

## PROCEDURE OUTLINE
This analysis explored the data in four hierarchical phases.
- 1.1. Data Standardization: Involved correcting spelling mistakes, enduring that each colum accurately reflects its data types. Data standardizing is the foremost step to ensuring that the data presented is accurate and consistent.
- 1.2. Data cleaning: Involved handling of missing values and duplicates. Case example in this project, there were cases of duplication and missing values. For each case, the duplicates were removed thereby ensuring that only distinct values remained. For the missing value at row 36 under Unit_Price column, data imputation was done using the average value (mean) obtained during analysis.
- 1.3. Data Analysis: Data analysis was the third phase, it involved conducting calculations on data so as to obtain answers to key business questions. The phase involved computing calculated columns, descriptive statistics, hypothesis testing and data modeling.
- 1.4. Reporting and Dashboards: Is almost the last phase. Use of Pivot tables to compute and summarize data in quicker way so as to create charts and visuals for presentations.

## FINDINGS
1. Wednesday as day of week and December as the month of the year had the highest sales in revenue.
2. 30% of customers use of bank transfer as the mode of payment when ordering products in the ecommerce site.
3. There is a statistically significant relationship between delivery time and the order status. In this case orders that take a long period of time to be delivered to respective customers are highly likely to be returned. 


## RECOMMENDATIONS
- The percentage of returned orders was at 48% compared to those of completed orders at 52%. This is almost a 50-50 ratio scenario where the e-commerce hub does not reach its maximum potential in sales revenues and profits. The 48% returned orders id due to the fact that products take longer on average of more than 7 days to be delivered its customers. Trademark Global is encouraged to drastically reduce the delivery period  so that customers receive their products in the shortest time possible. Measures could include employing more delivery personnel across their stores around the world!
